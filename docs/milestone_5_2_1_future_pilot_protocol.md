# SemanticFlow Milestone 5.2.1 Future Pilot Protocol

## Pilot A — instrumentation

Objective: validate acquisition behavior, paid screening, provenance, actual costs, disagreement diagnostics, and restart safety. It cannot establish formal quality qualification. Expected 280 requests, ceiling 360, expected proxy $0.004174.

## Pilot B — formal qualification

Not recommended for the current Banking77 and Beauty workloads. A design with 95% probability of merely obtaining 153 positives requires an estimated 61,365 physical requests, 110,868 named evaluations, $0.901208 proxy, and $1.351811 conservative proxy. Expected representative counts and costs are too large relative to execution savings, and a plan at exactly the 95% recall threshold has very low certification power. No paid schedule is authorized.

## Pilot C — exploratory risk validation

Objective: independently evaluate a frozen risk predictor on fresh calibration/test partitions without guarantee claims. Expected 2200 requests, ceiling 3300, expected proxy $0.032797.

## Independent evaluation freeze

Freeze policy hashes, alpha allocation, checkpoints, candidates, sampling, stopping, estimator, schedule, and dataset partitions before fresh outcomes. Human annotation is a separate study. Every pilot requires a new exact authorization; historical authorization cannot be reused.
